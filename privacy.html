AI Studio – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

AI Studio ("the app") is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload their own videos to their own TikTok

account.

  1. Data collected. The app does not collect, sell or share personal data of any other person. It only uses the access token that TikTok gives when the owner authorizes the app, and the basic profile information needed to show which account is connected.
  2. Storage. The access token is stored only on the owner's own computer, in a private file. It is never sent to any server other than TikTok.
  3. Use. The data is used only to upload the owner's videos to their TikTok account.
  4. Third parties. No data is shared with third parties.
  5. Deletion. The owner can revoke access at any time from TikTok settings (Security > Manage app permissions) and delete the local file.
  6. Changes. This policy may be updated; the date above shows the latest version.
